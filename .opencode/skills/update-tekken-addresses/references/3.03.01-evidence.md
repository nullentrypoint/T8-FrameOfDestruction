# Reference: Migration from 3.02.02 to 3.03.01

These are historical findings, not instructions to reuse the same offsets for
the next patch. Read current values from the INI and validate them against live
memory. Large captures and temporary analysis scripts are not bundled with this skill.

## Evidence and limitations

- Selected characters Jin/Kazuya had IDs 6/8. Sequential frame counters were
  verified across 32 blocks, followed by a new PID/heap after process restart.
- Jin's jab produced +1 on block and +8 on normal/counter hit. The recovery/timer
  formula matched publications in 73 block and 65 hit samples from one capture
  pair, and in 83 P2 block samples. These are not future balance constants.
- On stance move 1897, actionable changed from -1 to 26 while recovery was 55.
  The defender's block recovery was 25. Using actionable gave -1 in 113 samples,
  while the direct FA publication field remained zero.
- The first capture named `f4f_block` actually contained hits. Outcome 12 rather
  than 1 exposed this mismatch. Filenames and intended guard settings do not
  substitute for checking recorded events.
- Outcome was 1 for block, 12 for normal hit, and 10 for counter hit. Stun
  `0x68c` was 0/256 in all three cases; GameLog only compared it with NONE.
  Historical enum names did not describe its complete observed semantics.
- Throw tag `0xE000001F` did not mean "button 2 only": metadata allowed 1 OR 2.
  Successful breaks changed the tag to `0xD000001C`, raised the acceptance byte,
  and subsequently transitioned to the cancel metadata target.
- Four automatic ordinary-throw breaks were already accepted on the first
  sampled connection frame; this did not measure human reaction time.
  Notification byte `0x23d9` stayed 1 between events and was unsuitable as a fresh
  acceptance signal.
- Counter byte `0x1c88` was positively verified for throws. It stayed 0 for
  counter-jabs; its meaning must not be generalized to every attack type.
- Dragunov's ground selector retained 1, reset to 0 on tackle entry, then became
  2 on defender button 2 and 3 on 1+2. The defender's selector stayed 0.
  Ground metadata contained `1AF:3,840D:1`. This capture did not include a
  successful ground break, so selector discovery did not validate the complete
  ground-break outcome path.
- Applying a naive contact offset delta of +0x80 reached a continuously changing
  body position. Correct offset `0x3620` remained cached between contacts; six
  P2-capture updates matched fresh blocks. The hitbox equaled contact on three
  active-phase samples. Projection produced plausible NDC coordinates, but visual
  pixel alignment had not been confirmed.
- A naive facing delta of +0x80 failed to track all reversals. Offset `0x3ab4`
  was verified through physical crossover and position reset while keeping P2
  selected.
- Five captures were replayed through production code in both orientations:
  48 emissions/updates had the expected FA values. Capture gaps prevented
  frame-perfect timing claims.

Another `SteamNetworking006` initializer existed at `0x697c180`, with context
`0x97c3610`. The interface-name string alone did not uniquely identify a candidate.
The production reader successfully read the local identity, but two-client
online audio remained unverified. The `steamclient64.dll` method guard at
`0x71e600` also remained unverified against an active networking context.

Later Fight Lounge and custom-room discoveries are documented separately in
`room-voice-addresses.md`, including the `VoiceLounge` / `VoiceCustomLobby`
sections, PE/vtable guards, member-map layouts and local-exit/restart evidence.
Subsequent two-client logs and user feedback confirmed bidirectional voice in
both room modes; this does not retroactively validate the optional peer-trace
hook or every match/spectator transition.
